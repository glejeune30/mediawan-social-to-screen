# 2. Benchmark: what existing trend tools miss

| Tool | What it does well | What it misses for Mediawan's decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Trends** | Free, long history, global | Measures mainstream search. A story shows up when it is already big. Relative scale only. |
| **Exploding Topics, Glimpse** | Detect fast-growing topics from search and social data | Generic topics (products, keywords), not stories or rights. No link to adaptation potential. |
| **TikTok Creative Center** | Trending hashtags, sounds and creators on TikTok | Hashtags, not titles. One platform only. Built for advertisers. |
| **Parrot Analytics** | Audience demand for TV shows and talent | Measures demand for content that already exists on screen, not source material before adaptation. |
| **Social listening suites (Brandwatch, Talkwalker)** | Deep monitoring of mentions | Expensive, brand-oriented, need you to know what to track. |
| **Bestseller lists (NYT, Amazon charts)** | Reliable sales signal | Lagging by design: a bestseller is already mainstream. |

## Our angle

- **One score per story**, not per hashtag or keyword.
- **Across communities**: a story that accelerates on several platforms at once is a stronger signal.
- **Timed to the rights window**: the score rewards titles that grow in niche communities *while the
  mainstream is still unaware*, which is when an option is cheapest.
- **Tested against real deals**: we measure how early the radar would have flagged past adaptations.
